Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical website project take?

Most standard websites are completed in 3–5 weeks from kickoff, depending on scope. E-commerce and custom builds typically run 5–8 weeks.

Can I switch packages later?

Yes. Packages are designed to scale with you — you can upgrade at any renewal as your marketing needs grow.

Do you work with businesses outside Pakistan?

Absolutely. We work with clients internationally and structure communication, reporting and timelines around your timezone.

What's included in monthly reporting?

You'll receive a clear breakdown of traffic, ad performance, rankings and conversions, along with our recommendations for the next month.

Is there a minimum contract length?

We recommend a 3-month minimum for marketing packages, since SEO and ads need time to compound — but there's no long-term lock-in beyond that.
