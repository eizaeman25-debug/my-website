Is teeth scaling painful or harmful to tooth enamel?

No! Professional ultrasonic scaling gently vibrates off surface plaque and calculus without damaging natural tooth enamel. Dr. Ahmed Hassan uses precise ultrasonic scalers to ensure maximum patient comfort.
